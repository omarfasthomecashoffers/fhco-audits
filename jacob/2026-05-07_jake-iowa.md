### Coaching Rule: "What" and "How" Trump Everything

When in doubt, start with **"What"** or **"How."**

- "What came up that made you want to sell now?"
- "How has this been affecting you?"
- "What happens if this doesn't sell in the next 60 days?"
- "How would that make you feel?"
- "What are you most concerned about?"
- "How do you see this playing out if nothing changes?"

These force the seller to self-diagnose and expand. Yes/no questions let them off the hook.
