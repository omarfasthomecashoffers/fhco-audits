Ask: "How inconvenient would it be if you had to deal with another property manager cycle? Do you think it's worth the risk?" The accusation audit (disappointed tone, "you'll probably still be a little disappointed by what they approved") is specifically designed to defuse that reaction before it lands. Skipping it on a high-gap offer means the rejection hits harder, the emotional stakes rise faster, and you lose control of the conversation at the exact moment you need it most. Three seconds of delivery. Never skip it on a low offer.
